# 3. The tide mills of the estuary

For most of their working life the mills ran on a simple bargain with the sea [@hale2018].
A pond filled on the rising tide, and the wheel turned as the water was let back out.

The last mill on the estuary closed in 1871, when grain began arriving by rail [@morrow2015].
